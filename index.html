What we treat

A focused set of
services, done well.

Rather than offer everything, we concentrate on the procedures we can deliver at a consistently high standard.

Preventive & hygiene

Routine exams, cleaning, and early diagnostics — the foundation visit we recommend twice a year.

Restorative care

Fillings, crowns, and root canal treatment, done with an emphasis on preserving natural structure.

Cosmetic dentistry

Whitening, veneers, and alignment consultations tailored to how a smile should look, not a template.

Implants

Single-tooth and full-arch implant planning, from consultation through to the final restoration.

Orthodontics

Clear aligners and traditional braces for adults and adolescents, with quarterly progress reviews.

Emergency visits

Same-day appointments held open daily for urgent pain, breakage, or trauma.
